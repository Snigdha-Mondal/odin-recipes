Chicken Sandwich

Description

These sandwiches are great when you want a quick supper, but not something from the freezer. The spread and the chicken can be made in advance, and the chicken can be eaten hot or cold.

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless chicken breast(sliced thin)
  • 4 bread slices
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced into rings
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 0.5 teaspoons black pepper
  • 0.5 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoons garlic powder
  • 1 cheese slices

Steps

  1. Season the chicken: Season 2 boneless chicken breast, sliced thin with 0.5 teaspoons salt, 0.5 teaspoons black pepper, and 1 teaspoons garlic powder.
  2. Cook the chicken: Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a pan and cook the chicken until golden and cooked through.
  3. Toast the bread: Toast 4 bread slices lightly until golden.
  4. Spread the mayo: Spread 2 tablespoons mayonnaise on the toasted bread slices.
  5. Combine pasta and sauce: Drain the pasta and toss it into the sauce, adding a splash of reserved pasta water to loosen if needed.
  6. Assemble the sandwich: Layer 1 lettuce leaves, 1 tomato, sliced, 1 onion, sliced into rings, the cooked chicken, and 1 cheese slices between the bread slices
  7. Slice and serve: Press gently, slice in half, and serve.
